Plain-English Answers
Who can see my family's information?
Only your account can directly access your household data. Your information is protected by row-level security — a technical safeguard that prevents even Folia staff from querying your family's profile. The service providers we use to operate Folia may receive limited information to do their specific jobs (like processing payments or generating your brief), but are not permitted to use it for any other purpose.
Do you sell our data?
No. Folia does not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We may disclose information to service providers that help us operate the service. These providers are permitted to use personal information only as needed to provide services to Folia, or as otherwise permitted by law.
Do you collect sensitive information?
You may choose to provide information in forms, generated briefs, PDFs, or optional notes. Please do not include sensitive personal information in optional notes or free-text fields unless it is necessary for your use of the service. We do not ask for health information or other highly sensitive information unless it is needed for a specific feature you choose to use, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect information you submit.
How do AI-powered features use my information?
If you use Folia's AI-powered features, we may send the content you submit and related instructions to Anthropic to generate drafts, summaries, or other outputs for you. Under Anthropic's current API terms, Anthropic does not use inputs or outputs from API services to train its AI models by default. Folia does not independently use your submitted content to train any AI model. Because Anthropic's terms are subject to change, we encourage you to review Anthropic's privacy practices at anthropic.com if you have questions.
Do you use analytics or tracking?
Folia uses Google Analytics to understand how users interact with the service (for example, which features are used and how often). Google Analytics collects information such as pages visited, time spent on pages, and general geographic region. We have disabled Google's advertising features, meaning your information is not used for ad targeting or cross-site tracking through this tool. You can learn more about how Google uses data at google.com/policies/privacy/partners.
Can I delete my data?
Yes. You can request deletion of your account or your personal information at any time by emailing privacy@withfolia.com. We will verify your identity before processing your request and will delete or de-identify your personal information within thirty (30) days. We may keep limited information where required by law, including for payments, tax, fraud prevention, security, and legal compliance.
Is Folia intended for children?
No. Folia is intended for a general adult audience. You must be at least 18 years old to create a Folia account. Folia is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can investigate and delete the information as appropriate.
Will you send me marketing emails?
Only if you opt in. If you check the opt-in box at Step 1 of the brief generator, we will send you a welcome email and occasional tips or updates about Folia. You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email we send. We will not send you marketing emails if you do not opt in, and opting out does not affect your ability to use the service.
What if there's a data breach?
We protect your information using encryption in transit (TLS/HTTPS), encryption at rest, access controls, and regular security reviews. No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your personal information and requires notice, we will notify you as required by law.

1. Who We Are

Folia is a brief generator and caregiver information service operated by AJS Family, LLC, a California limited liability company doing business as Folia ("Folia," "we," "us," or "our"). Our website is withfolia.com. You can reach us with privacy questions at privacy@withfolia.com.

2. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Folia collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information when you visit our website, use the Folia brief generator, or create a Folia account. It also describes your rights with respect to your personal information.

By using Folia, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use Folia.

This Privacy Policy is incorporated into and forms part of Folia's Terms of Service, available at withfolia.com/terms.

3. What Folia Does

Folia is a brief generator that allows users to enter household and caregiver information and generate informational briefs and PDFs for personal, non-commercial household use. Folia is not a medical records system, a healthcare provider, or a substitute for professional medical, legal, financial, or other advice of any kind.

4. Information We Collect

a. Information You Provide Directly

When you use Folia, you may provide:

  • Account information: Your name, email address, and password when you create an account.
  • Brief and form content: Household information, schedules, caregiver instructions, contact details, and other content you enter to generate briefs or PDFs.
  • Optional notes: If you use optional free-text or notes fields, you decide what information to include. Folia strongly recommends against entering sensitive personal information in free-text fields, including health or medical details, financial account information, or government identification numbers. Folia also includes dedicated optional fields for home network and security credential information (such as wifi network name and password and home security or access codes); entry of information in those fields is entirely voluntary, and we strongly recommend entering security credentials only when strictly necessary for the specific caregiving engagement described in the brief. If you choose to include sensitive information in any field, you do so entirely at your own risk and discretion, and you are solely responsible for its accuracy, completeness, and currency.
  • Payment information: If you subscribe to a paid plan, you provide payment information to Stripe, our payment processor. Folia does not store full payment card numbers on its own servers. We may receive limited transaction information from Stripe, such as payment status, billing name, and the last four digits of a card, for billing, records, and support purposes.
  • Communications: If you contact us by email or otherwise, we collect the content of those communications.

b. Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website or use the service, we and our service providers may automatically collect:

  • Usage data: Pages visited, features used, brief completions, and similar activity data.
  • Device and browser data: Browser type, operating system, IP address, and referring URLs.
  • Analytics data: We use Google Analytics to collect aggregated traffic and usage data. Google's advertising features and data-sharing settings are disabled. We do not send personally identifiable information to Google Analytics.

c. Information We Do Not Solicit

Folia does not solicit, prompt for, or maintain dedicated fields for health information, medical data, financial account numbers, or government identification numbers. Folia is not a medical records system and does not treat any information you enter as medical data. Folia does include dedicated fields for home network and security credential information, including wifi network name and password and home security or access codes, because this information is often necessary for caregiving engagements. Entry of this information is entirely optional. We strongly recommend that you enter security credentials only when strictly necessary for the specific caregiving engagement described in the brief. If you choose to include such information, you do so at your own risk and are solely responsible for any consequences, including unauthorized access to your home network or property. If you use optional free-text or notes fields to enter other sensitive information, you do so entirely at your own risk. Our Terms of Service disclaim liability for information you choose to enter voluntarily, and you are solely responsible for ensuring that any information you enter and share with caregivers is accurate, complete, and up to date.

d. Do Not Track

California's Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA) requires us to disclose how we respond to web browser "Do Not Track" (DNT) signals. At this time, there is no universally accepted standard for responding to DNT signals, and Folia does not currently alter its data collection or use practices in response to DNT signals from your browser. We do not authorize third parties to collect personal information about individual users' online activities over time and across third-party websites through our service. If our practices change, we will update this Privacy Policy accordingly.

8. Children's Privacy

Folia is intended for adults and is not directed to children. You must be at least 18 years old to create a Folia account. Children under 13 may not create accounts. All information about minor household members is entered and controlled exclusively by adult account holders. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13, and we do not market to children. Our practices are designed to be consistent with the principles of the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Information About Minor Household Members. Because Folia's brief generator may be used to document schedules, pick-up and drop-off locations, emergency contacts, and caregiving instructions for minor children, account holders should exercise particular care when entering information about minors. We strongly recommend: (i) entering only the minimum information necessary to generate a useful and accurate brief; (ii) avoiding entry of specific home addresses, school names or addresses, health or medical information, or other sensitive identifying details about minors in any free-text or notes fields; (iii) reviewing each generated brief in its entirety before sharing it with any caregiver to confirm it does not contain sensitive identifying information about a minor beyond what is necessary for the specific caregiving engagement; and (iv) sharing generated briefs only directly and privately with the specific authorized caregiver for the engagement described in that brief, and never distributing or posting a brief containing information about a minor in any public, group, or uncontrolled channel. Information about minor children in caregiving briefs — including location, schedule, and emergency contact data — is sensitive. You are solely responsible for controlling to whom you provide any brief that contains information about a minor household member.

If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, please contact us at privacy@withfolia.com. If we learn that we have collected personal information directly from a child under 13 without appropriate authorization, we will take steps to delete that information promptly. Folia does not independently review, moderate, or restrict the type or volume of information entered about minor household members and cannot prevent account holders from entering sensitive information about minors. You are solely responsible for ensuring that any information you enter about a minor is limited to what is necessary for the intended caregiving purpose, is accurate and current, and is shared only with individuals you have specifically authorized to provide care.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide the service, fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, and comply with our legal obligations. When you request deletion of your account, we will delete or de-identify your personal information within thirty (30) days, subject to information we are required or permitted to retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention, tax, accounting, billing, dispute resolution, or compliance purposes. For requests submitted pursuant to your rights under Section 12, we will provide a formal response within forty-five (45) days of receipt, consistent with the CCPA/CPRA.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your personal information, including encryption in transit (such as TLS/HTTPS), encryption at rest, and access controls designed to limit access to authorized personnel and service providers. We also implement row-level security at the database level, a technical control that ensures only your account can access your family's data — Folia staff and other users cannot query your household information. No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information.

If we determine that a security breach affecting your personal information requires notification, we will notify you as required by applicable law, including as required by California Civil Code § 1798.82, which requires notification in the most expedient time possible and without unreasonable delay.

Folia is a tool that generates informational briefs based solely on the information you provide. You are solely responsible for the accuracy, completeness, and currency of all information you enter into Folia. Folia does not verify, validate, or independently confirm any information you submit. Briefs generated by Folia reflect only the information you have entered at the time of generation and may not reflect subsequent changes to household circumstances, caregiver instructions, health status, or other relevant information. Each generated brief includes the date and time of generation; it is your responsibility to provide caregivers with the most recently generated brief and to update your information before each caregiving engagement.

Data Accuracy and Profile Integrity. Where Folia provides individual household member profiles or similar features, it is your sole responsibility to verify, before sharing any generated brief with any caregiver, that all information in that brief has been correctly assigned to the intended household member. Folia does not guarantee that data entered for one household member will not be displayed, attributed, or associated with another household member as a result of user error, software error, or any other cause. YOU ARE SOLELY RESPONSIBLE FOR REVIEWING EACH GENERATED BRIEF IN ITS ENTIRETY BEFORE PROVIDING IT TO ANY CAREGIVER TO CONFIRM THAT ALL INFORMATION IS ACCURATELY ASSIGNED TO THE CORRECT INDIVIDUAL. No caregiver should act on any brief without the account holder first verbally confirming all critical details, including but not limited to pick-up and drop-off locations, emergency contacts, and any care instructions.

Folia expressly disclaims all liability for any harm, loss, or damage arising from: (i) inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated information entered by you; (ii) a caregiver's reliance on a brief that has not been updated to reflect current information; (iii) any discrepancy between information contained in a generated brief and actual household conditions; (iv) any mis-assignment, co-mingling, or incorrect attribution of data between household member profiles, whether caused by user error, software error, or any other cause; (v) any error, omission, or distortion in AI-generated output that does not accurately reflect the information you submitted; (vi) a caregiver's failure to independently verify information directly with you before providing care; or (vii) any brief shared with or accessed by an unintended third party as a result of your actions. You should review and update your briefs regularly and instruct any caregiver to verify all details directly with you before providing care.
