Reel 5 — Mental Load of Motherhood (dark)
"The mental load of motherhood is real. And the worst part isn't the work — it's the invisible carrying. The constant remembering. The being the only one who knows. What if something else could hold it? Not a person. Not a spreadsheet. Something that actually learns your family and keeps it all current automatically. That's what I built. It's called Folia. Withfolia.com."
Reel 6 — Same Info Every Time (dark)
"New teacher. New sitter. New camp. New doctor. And every single time — the same conversation. The allergy. The medication. The routine. The emergency contacts. From memory. Again. I got so tired of saying the same things over and over to every new person who entered my children's lives that I built something about it. Folia gives every caregiver exactly what they need — instantly — and keeps it current so you never have to repeat yourself again. Withfolia.com."
