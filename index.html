What Folia Is

Not a Google Doc.
A living record — for your whole family.

Most families already have a system — a shared doc, a group chat, a folder someone made two years ago. The problem isn't organization. It's that nothing stays current.

i.

Profiles that stay current

Each person in your household has their own profile. When something changes — a new pickup time, a new caregiver — update it once and everyone who needs it sees the new version instantly.

ii.

Calendar sync Coming soon

Folia is built to connect to Google Calendar. When a school event gets rescheduled or a practice is cancelled, it will flag the update automatically. Founding families get this first.

iii.

Inbox sync Coming soon

When the school emails about an early release or a cancelled practice, Folia will catch it and ask if you want to update — one tap, done. Rolling out to founding families first.

iv.

Grocery learning Coming soon

Folia is designed to learn from your grocery orders — building your recurring list automatically and keeping your family's dietary profile accurate. Coming to founding families soon.

v.

Share instantly

When the sitter arrives, send her everything she needs in one tap — pickup time, allergy details, bedtime routine, emergency contacts. No retyping. No forgetting. Just send.

vi.

You're always in control

Nothing in Folia changes without your approval. Every update is shown to you first. You decide what gets saved. Your family's information stays private, secure, and exactly as accurate as you want it.

Why It's Different

It's not about storing information.
It's about never having
to update it again.

A Google Doc is passive. You make it with good intentions, share it with everyone who needs it, and within a month it's out of date. You know the one. It has the old pediatrician's number and a pickup schedule from last school year.

Folia is designed to be active. It's built to connect to your email, your calendar, and your grocery orders — so that when something changes, it catches it and asks you to confirm. That's what makes it different from a notes app. Those connections are rolling out to founding families now — and you can be one of them.
